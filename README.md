Software Engineering & AI student at Dakota State University (May 2027) who likes shipping things people can actually use.

- **Bimo**: a RAG app that turns any document into a chatbot that answers only from that document → [live demo](https://bimo.iamola.net/)
- **Reffa**: a cross-platform app for Resident Assistants, published on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reffa/id6748703800) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ohla.Reffa)
- Built biometric and card-based login systems in C# and C++ as a research assistant at DSU's AdapT Lab
- I deploy my own work with Docker and NGINX on a DigitalOcean VPS

Outside of code: former Student Senate President, co-founder of a campus charity, and peer tutor in CS & math.
